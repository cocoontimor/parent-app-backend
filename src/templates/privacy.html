Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Cocoon ("Cocoon", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information when you use the Cocoon parent-engagement platform (the "Service"). Cocoon is used by our preschool in Dili, Timor-Leste to keep parents and guardians informed about their children.

Because the Service handles information about children, we take particular care with that data. Please read this policy carefully. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the Service.

1. Who we are

The Service is operated by Cocoon, a preschool in Dili, Timor-Leste. We are the data controller responsible for your personal information. The Cocoon platform is built and maintained on our behalf by our contractor, Intuitive (see Section 6).

Contact for privacy questions: support@cocoontimor.org

2. Who uses the Service

  • Parents and guardians — receive messages about their child, view a read-only web app, complete e-learning lessons, and confirm fee payments.
  • Staff and teachers — post announcements and child updates, manage classes and family circles, and log fee payments.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Information about parents and guardians

  • Phone number — your WhatsApp phone number is your account identifier and the way we send you messages and sign-in links.
  • Name — your full name.
  • Email address — only if provided (optional; not required to use the Service).
  • Relationship to a child — e.g. mother, father, guardian.
  • Family and classroom groups ("circles") you belong to.

3.2 Information about children

We collect the following about each enrolled child, provided by the school and/or by you:

  • Name
  • Date of birth
  • Photos of the child, included in updates and announcements.
  • Updates and notes written by staff about the child's day, activities, and wellbeing.
  • Enrollment details — which family and classroom groups the child belongs to, and whether the child has graduated.
  • School-fee records associated with the child — the month and amount of fees logged by staff.

3.3 Messages and activity

  • Messages we send you over WhatsApp — announcements, child updates, urgent alerts, daily digests, e-learning lessons, fee confirmations, and sign-in links — including their full content and delivery status.
  • Messages you send us over WhatsApp — the message text and technical details WhatsApp provides about the message.
  • E-learning activity — which lessons you have marked as watched, and when.
  • Acknowledgements — when you confirm you have seen an announcement or alert.
  • Sign-in and session data — the times you sign in and technical session information needed to keep you logged in.

We do not collect payment card or bank account details. School fees are recorded manually by staff; the Service does not process card or bank payments.

4. How we use your information

We use personal information to:

  • Send parents and guardians updates, announcements, alerts, daily digests, and fee confirmations about their children;
  • Deliver e-learning lessons and track lesson progress;
  • Authenticate you and keep you signed in (via a WhatsApp sign-in link);
  • Allow staff to manage classes, family circles, and enrollment;
  • Record and confirm school-fee payments;
  • Operate, maintain, secure, and improve the Service.

We use children's information solely to provide the above services to that child's parents, guardians, and the school. We do not use it for advertising or profiling, and we do not sell it.

5. Legal basis for processing

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we rely on:

  • Performance of our agreement with the school and with parents to deliver the Service;
  • Legitimate interests in operating and securing the Service and communicating with parents about their children;
  • Consent, where required — for example, for taking and sharing photographs of children. Consent for a child's data is provided by a parent or guardian.

6. How we share information — third-party services

We share personal information only as needed to run the Service:

  • Intuitive — our contractor, which builds, hosts, and maintains the Cocoon platform on our behalf and operates the WhatsApp business number used to reach you. Intuitive processes personal information only under our instructions to run the Service.
  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business / Cloud API) — all messages we send to and receive from you, including their content and your phone number, are transmitted through WhatsApp's infrastructure and are subject to WhatsApp's and Meta's privacy terms. This includes message content that may reference a child's name, updates about the child, and fee amounts.
  • Google Cloud (Google Cloud Platform) — we host our database and store uploaded photos and files using Google Cloud services (Cloud SQL and Cloud Storage).
  • YouTube (Google) — e-learning lessons link to videos hosted on YouTube; when you watch a lesson, your interaction takes place on YouTube and is subject to Google's privacy policy.

We share information with these providers only to operate the Service. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for third-party advertising.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of children, parents, staff, or the Service.

7. International transfers

The providers above (Meta and Google) operate globally, so your information — including children's information — may be processed on servers outside Timor-Leste. Where we transfer data internationally, we rely on the providers' contractual and technical safeguards to protect it.

8. Data retention

We retain personal information for as long as a child is enrolled and an account is active, and as needed to operate the Service and meet legal or record-keeping obligations. When information is no longer needed, we will delete it or de-identify it. You may request deletion as described in Section 10.

9. How we protect information

We use technical and organizational measures to protect personal information, including encrypted database connections and access controls that limit information to authorized staff. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your rights and choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you and your child;
  • Correct inaccurate information;
  • Request deletion of your or your child's information;
  • Object to or restrict certain processing;
  • Withdraw consent (for example, for photographs) at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@cocoontimor.org. As a parent or guardian, you may exercise these rights on behalf of your child. We will respond within the time required by applicable law.

You can stop receiving WhatsApp messages at any time by contacting the school or your child's teacher.

11. Children's privacy

The Service is designed for use by adults (parents, guardians, and staff) to share information about children. Children do not create accounts or use the Service directly. All information about a child is provided and managed by the school and by the child's parents or guardians. We collect and use children's information only to provide the Service to their families and school, and never for marketing.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify parents through the Service or over WhatsApp.
